## Feature Design

## Approach

The features were derived from user research insights, personas, and behavioral assumptions identified during the analysis phase.

The focus was on connecting financial actions with emotional context to better support user reflection.

---

## Core Features

- Expense tracking with emotional tagging
- Calendar-based overview of spending behavior
- Identification of potentially impulsive purchases
- Financial summaries over time
- Pattern recognition in emotional spending behavior

---

## Design Principle

The system is not only a tracking tool, but also a reflection-oriented system.

It is designed to support users in:

- reflecting on emotional financial decisions
- recognizing behavioral patterns over time
- increasing awareness of spending behavior

---

## Key Concept

Emotional context is treated as a core factor in understanding financial behavior rather than as optional metadata.

This enables a more reflective interpretation of financial activity.
