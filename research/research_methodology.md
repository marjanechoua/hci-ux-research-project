# Research Phase

This folder contains all research artifacts from the early phase of the Refy project.

The goal of this phase was to understand user behavior related to financial decision-making, with a focus on emotional and digital influences.

## Contents

- survey_results.md → quantitative findings from user survey
- interview_summary.md → qualitative insights from interviews
- research_insights.md → synthesized findings and key takeaways

## Outcome

The research directly informed:

- persona creation
- feature definition
- system design decisions
